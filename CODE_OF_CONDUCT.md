# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-led research
community. Participants must communicate professionally, welcome questions,
criticise ideas rather than people, acknowledge uncertainty, and give proper
credit for scientific and software contributions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and restrict participation when conduct undermines a safe and constructive
community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel published on the
GitHub repository. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
